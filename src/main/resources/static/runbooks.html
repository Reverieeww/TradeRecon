Investigation runbook · v1

Project-approved deterministic guidance for synthetic data. Not financial advice or a bank operating procedure. These steps cannot establish an unsupported root cause.

RB-FIELDS · Different business fields

Compare all highlighted fields to original source records, including account/instrument identifiers and currency. Ask the source owner for evidence supporting a correction. Do not guess which source is right or offset differences across currencies.

RB-VERSIONS · Incomplete or invalid version chain

Check NEW revision 1 and every following revision. Request missing revisions or conflicting delivery evidence. A higher revision alone is not sufficient. Preserve rejected files and previous accepted history; do not rewrite events.

RB-MISSING · Missing counterpart

Check accepted receipt time, ten-minute wait boundary, source delivery logs and both completeness declarations. A missing record is not proof that no trade occurred. Corrected files affect a new run, not the old snapshot.

RB-CANCEL · Cancellation

Compare both cancellation notices and their previous valid values. A cancellation must preserve the previous business payload. Do not invent a reason for cancellation or reclassify a manual closure as matching data.

RB-MATCHED · Matching fields

The seven compared business fields agree within this frozen snapshot. Independently check feed completeness and rejected input. Two feeds both omitting a trade cannot be detected without another authoritative source.
